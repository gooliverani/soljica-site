The menu

A short pick of what we eat and drink around here. The full menu is waiting at the bar.

From the kitchen

Šoljica breakfast

2 eggs, bacon, sausage, house-made cheese and sesame pastries, crostini with avocado feta and sunflower seeds, cherry tomatoes, arugula and a honey-mustard dressing.

Scrambled eggs on avocado

Creamy scrambled eggs, avocado and crispy smoked pork neck (or without it) on toasted bread.

Croque madame

Buđola on rustic bread with clarified butter, covered in an aromatic béchamel, baked with cheddar and organic eggs, fresh chives.

Danish sandwich

Smoked salmon in a garlic-and-dill cheese spread, on our rye bread, with avocado, cucumber, capers, orange, fermented red onion and sunflower seeds.

Club sandwich

Chicken, our special mayo-based spread, tomato, cheese, lettuce and three slices of toast.

Šoljica Smash Burger

Beef burger in a brioche bun with cheddar, burger sauce, coleslaw, red onion, jalapeño, tomato, pickles and bacon, served with fries, onion rings, cheddar sauce and ketchup.

Caesar salad

Chicken, butterhead lettuce, Caesar dressing, croutons, mushrooms, bacon, parmesan, cherry tomatoes.

Šoljica Waffle

A single or double waffle with our vanilla cream and forest fruit, apricot, white and dark chocolate toppings.
